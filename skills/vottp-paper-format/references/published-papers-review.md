# Three recent published papers

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Selection: three computing-related papers from the latest
listed issue, **№3 (2026), published 2026-09-10**, including two Ukrainian research
papers and one English review. This is a purposive formatting sample, not evidence
that all accepted papers follow identical rules. Full PDF text was read; all three
first pages and the session-memory paper's results-table page were visually
inspected. Page sizes/fonts were measured with Poppler and PyMuPDF.

## Observed layout versus submission requirements

All three PDFs have A4 pages (~595.44 × 841.92 pt), one body column, right-aligned
author blocks, centered bold titles and main headings, bilingual abstracts/keywords
before the body, and bracketed numbered citations. The body is Times New Roman
~10.08 pt; titles and author names are 12 pt; affiliations/contacts and much of the
References text are ~7.92 pt. Abstract spans use Tahoma ~7.92 pt with an italic
appearance. Sizes are PDF measurements, not a new precision requirement for Word.

These observations support the **optional published profile**:

| Element | Observed convention | Skill decision |
|---|---|---|
| UDC | Upper left, preceding right-aligned authors | Follow guide placement; preserve author-confirmed value. |
| Authors | Surname uppercase, given name; right-aligned; local-language contact block then translated names/affiliation | Preserve supplied spellings; offer uppercase surname as a formatting/text proposal, not an identity correction. |
| Titles | Bold uppercase, centered, 12 pt | In default guide profile use 10 pt; optional published profile uses 12 pt. Uppercasing requires author-approved text edits. |
| Abstracts/keywords | Justified compact paragraphs; UA→EN for UA body, EN→UA for EN body | Keep both ahead of body. Default follows guide 10 pt/1.3; optional profile uses Tahoma 8 pt italic/single. |
| Body | Justified, indented paragraphs; centered bold headings | Use the five content functions, allowing equivalent headings. Alignment/indent are local conventions. |
| Tables/figures | Session-memory PDF: table label above/right, title above/center; figure caption below/center | Use sequential numbering and prior text callouts; guide only explicitly requires placement in text. Do not invent a fixed float count. |
| Bibliography | One paper has two lists; two have References only | Prefer guide's two-list requirement; flag redundancy for all-Latin sources. |
| Production furniture | Journal header/footer, page number, assigned DOI, received/accepted/published dates, license strip | Publisher elements; never manufacture them in an author manuscript. |

PDFs cannot establish Word paragraph styles, exact line-spacing settings, original
submission margins, editorial waivers, or whether policies changed after submission.
Do not reverse-engineer these as mandatory rules. In particular, the PDFs show
several shorter lines and font substitutions; the helper's guide profile follows
the written 1.3 setting rather than attempting pixel-identical publisher pagination.

## Consequences for the skill

1. Give written instructions priority over sample deviations, and retain explicit
   conflict labels for author count, length and text size.
2. Use bilingual front matter at the start, with order matching manuscript language.
3. Preserve native equations and embedded media; scientific font runs such as Symbol
   and math must not be converted into ordinary Times New Roman letters.
4. Inspect populated results and claim support, not only headings and table counts.
5. Never treat a published example as evidence that an unsupported claim, missing
   value, absent list, or shorter manuscript will be accepted again.
