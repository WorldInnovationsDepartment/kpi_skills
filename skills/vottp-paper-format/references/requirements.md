## Content review depth

Check what the paper actually demonstrates: stated contribution, differences from
prior work, traceable method/algorithm, inputs/data, configurations and baselines,
measured results, uncertainty where appropriate, limits, and conclusions supported
by those results. For a review, examine selection/synthesis method and the new
analytical contribution rather than demanding a fabricated experiment. These are
practical assessments of C01–C03 and C's rejection reasons, not extra formal section
or statistical-test mandates. Report author-required scientific work separately from
formatting changes.
