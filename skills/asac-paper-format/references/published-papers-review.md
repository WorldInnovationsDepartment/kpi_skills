# Review of three recently published ASAC papers

Purpose: see how the editors actually typeset accepted papers, how strictly the author
guide (`requirements.md`) is enforced, and which choices the skill should copy.
Reviewed 2026-10-23 from the journal's PDFs (Wayback copies of asac.kpi.ua). Page
numbers refer to the printed issue.

## Paper 1: Gavrylenko & Zinchenko (EN, 13 pp.)

1. **Title block**: exactly as in the sample. "UDC 519.688" flush left; "**O. Gavrylenko,
   L. Zinchenko**" bold, right-aligned; title bold, centred, in capitals over two lines.
2. **Abstract under the title**: ~230 words in five unlabeled paragraphs that follow the
   structure (problem, purpose, method, results, conclusion). That is a full structured
   abstract without part labels, not the sample's 150-300 characters.
   "*Keywords*:" lists 5 phrases.
3. **Sections**: Introduction, Materials and methods, the results, "Discussion of
   results", Conclusions, REFERENCES. This is full IMRaD and the closest of the three
   to the guide. Sub-headings ("Emotional score computation") are bold.
4. **Introduction**: about 1.5 pages, cites [1-10], and ends with the aim of the work
   and the planned future use. It has no figures or tables.
5. **Formulas**: Word equations (Cambria Math), numbered (1)-(22) at the right margin,
   with groups of short formulas on consecutive lines.
6. **Tables**: "*Table 3.* **Extracted feature values and emotion scores**" on one
   centred line above the table, with a bold title. This differs from the sample's
   right-aligned label plus a centred title on a separate line. The table is fully
   ruled, with bold row labels for the scores.
7. **References**: 10 entries from 2005-2025, 7 of them from 2023-2025, and **all with
   DOIs**. Authors are in *italics*, but the entries are APA-like ("*Li, S., Deng, W.*
   Deep facial expression recognition: A survey. IEEE Transactions on Affective
   Computing, 2023. https://doi.org/...") rather than DSTU 8302.
8. **Against the guide**: the length of 13 pages exceeds 10 and needed editorial
   consent. Citations are numbered in order of first appearance. The reference format
   is not DSTU.

## Paper 2: Мамедова, Губський, Березін, Величко (UA, 6 pp.)

1. **Title block**: as in the sample ("УДК 004.657", bold right-aligned authors, a
   three-line upper-case title).
2. **Abstract under the title**: only ~50 words in two sentences (problem and aim),
   unstructured. The "*Ключові слова:*" line has **3** items: "Google Classroom
   API / OAuth 2.0, мапінг (зіставлення) даних, електронний кампус".
3. **Sections**: Вступ, Постановка проблеми (object, subject and aim of the work), then
   descriptive implementation sections, Висновки, and СПИСОК ВИКОРИСТАНИХ ДЖЕРЕЛ in
   capitals. There are no separate Materials and methods, Results or Discussion
   sections.
4. **Figures**: four diagrams (ER model, BPMN process) captioned below, centred, as
   "*Рисунок 2.* Фрагмент ER-моделі ..." with an italic label. This matches the sample.
5. **Conclusions**: **one sentence**, against the 5-10 the guide asks for, and with no
   future-work statement.
6. **References**: **3 entries** (2020, 2020, 2012) in an ad-hoc "Authors. Title. Year,
   Journal. DOI: ..." format, all with DOIs.
7. **Against the guide**: too few keywords and references, the conclusions are too short,
   the introduction is not literature-based, and the references are not DSTU. The paper
   was still published, so editorial enforcement is lenient. The skill should still aim
   for full compliance, because reviewers can cite any of these points.

## Paper 3: Stelmashenko & Deveciogullari (EN, 8 pp.)

1. **Title block**: as in the sample, with the title on one line.
2. **Abstract under the title**: **122 words** in one unstructured paragraph, followed by
   6 keywords ("software cost estimation, fuzzy logic, decision support, uncertainty,
   project management, interval forecasting").
3. **Sections**: Introduction, "Literature review and problem statement", a method
   section, "Experimental Study", Conclusion (singular), REFERENCES.
4. **Formulas**: numbered (1)-(4) and more at the right margin, with explanations after
   them. Membership functions are given as a bulleted list with "–" dashes.
5. **Figure**: "Figure 1. The architecture of the forecasting model", centred below the
   figure.
6. **References**: 8 entries in **DSTU style** ("Chawla R., Ahlawat D., Kumar M.
   Software Development Effort Estimation Techniques: A Review // International Journal
   ... – 2014. – Vol. 5, No. 5. – P. 1166–1170. – URL: ... (application date:
   05.05.2025)."). Only 1-2 have DOIs, and several are web pages (Medium, Wikipedia,
   GeeksforGeeks), which is weak for "international journals of the last 5 years".
7. **Against the guide**: the length is fine; the reference list is short, mostly web
   sources and mostly older than 5 years; the abstract is below 200 words and
   unstructured.

(An extra check of Д. Луцак, М. Ткач, «Формалізація задачі інтелектуального
голосового управління БПЛА», № 1 (48) 2026, pp. 11-19, showed the same pattern. It
has a 138-word abstract, descriptive headings and 9 references in mixed formats. Its
table caption is "*Таблиця 1.*" right-aligned with the title centred below it, exactly
as in the sample.)

## What the skill takes from this

1. **Follow the sample for typesetting.** All four papers use its title block, heading
   style, figure captions, equation numbering and A4 / TNR 13 / 1.3 look. The editors
   re-typeset the final layout (running header "Міжвідомчий науково-технічний збірник
   ... № N (NN) YYYY", "ISSN 1560-8956" and page numbers in the footer, "© Authors"
   footnote), so **authors must not add headers, footers, page numbers or the ©
   line**. The builder adds none.
2. **The abstract under the title is a full abstract in practice.** The skill repeats the
   structured abstract there without part labels, as in Paper 1, and keeps the labelled
   200-250-word versions for the end matter.
3. **Enforcement is lenient, but reviewers are not.** Short conclusions, 3 references,
   APA-style lists and 3 keywords all got through. The skill still flags each deviation,
   so the author decides knowingly rather than by accident.
4. **The strongest paper (Paper 1)** has IMRaD sections, a literature-based introduction
   that ends with the aim, numbered equations, ruled tables, 7 of 10 references recent,
   and DOIs everywhere. Use it as the model, but put the references into DSTU 8302:2015
   form.
5. **The length limit is soft at the top.** 13 pages was accepted with editorial
   consent. Below 5 pages there is no precedent, so treat it as a hard minimum.
